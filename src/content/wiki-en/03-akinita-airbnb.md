### Renting via Airbnb / Booking from abroad

If you own a property in Greece and rent it out on a short-term basis, you are required to:

**a) Register with the Short-Term Stay Register (AADE)**
- Obtain a **Property Registry Number (A.M.A.)** electronically via myAADE
- The A.M.A. must be **mandatorily** displayed on every platform (Airbnb, Booking, etc.)

**b) Submit a Short-Term Stay Declaration**
- Submit electronically for **each individual lease/booking**
- Finalization of the Registry: by the **end of February** each year

**c) Income Tax Return**
- Form **E2** (rentals) → subsequently transferred to **E1** (income tax return)

> ⚠️ **If you have a [Golden Visa](/en/wiki/10-golden-visa):** Golden Visa properties are **not** allowed to be rented out on a short-term basis (Airbnb). Only long-term leasing (at least 6 months) is permitted.

### Purchasing Property as a Resident Abroad

A resident abroad **is not subject to wealth criteria (tekmiria)** for purchasing real estate or a car in Greece, provided they have already been certified as a foreign tax resident.

For the transfer of real estate, the following are required: a Greek Tax Identification Number (AFM), a [tax representative](/en/wiki/01-forologiki-katoikia) or a digital declaration of acceptance of service, and a [tax residency certificate](/en/wiki/01-forologiki-katoikia).

### ENFIA 2026

The surcharges on the objective values in certain zones (over €2,800) may increase your tax. Check the [recent changes in wealth criteria and objective values](https://www.e-forosimv.gr/details.asp?ID=78805) to budget your installments.

### Land Registry (Ktimatologio)

For residents abroad, the deadline for submitting declarations is [six months](https://www.ktimatologio.gr/pliroforiako-yliko/ktimatografisi/7) from the start of collection in each area. Late declarations now result in tiered fines.
